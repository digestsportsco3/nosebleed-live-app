MARY’S MARVELOUS AMAGANSETT IS SHUT; ORGANIC KRUSH TO OPEN
Last December came the sad news that the lease for Mary’s Marvelous in Amagansett, represented by Lori Macgarva of Douglas Elliman, was up for sale. As of a couple days ago, the beloved cafe’s windows were sheathed in brown paper, and now comes the news that the lease, listed for $575K, has sold. Read more at BehindtheHedges.com
