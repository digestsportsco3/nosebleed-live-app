Title: Mary's Marvelous Amagansett is Shut; Organic Krush to Open (Behind The Hedges, Laura Euler, June 15, 2017)

Posted on June 15, 2017 

Last December came the sad news that the lease for Mary’s Marvelous in Amagansett, represented by Lori Macgarva of Douglas Elliman, was up for sale. As of a couple days ago, the beloved cafe’s windows were sheathed in brown paper, and now comes the news that the lease, listed for $575K, has sold.

The new business will be the organic cafe Organic Krush. They already have one business in Lake Success; looks like they’re all about the cold-pressed juices, breakfasts, acai bowls, and more. Sounds like they’ll be very successful in their new location.

And fear not, Mary’s fans, the Newtown Lane, East Hampton, cafe remains open.
