It's a Love Story, Baby Just Say Yes
We’ve always been a yes company. From the day we opened our doors in 2015, it was a big giant “yes” to the world that we would serve food that made people feel amazing. We were touring the East Coast, going to Taylor Swift concerts with our teen daughters and we could not find acceptable fast food anywhere. Food without hormones, GMO’s, & pesticides (those were our NO’s :). So we realized if it didn’t exist, we’d have to build it ourselves. After all, if Taylor Swift could make love music that made you cry, we could cook organic food that made you fly. Come krush with us on our journey to true health, we promise you will love the tour.
Our Mission: Serve organic food. Inspire a higher level of health. Share it with our communities

Frequently Asked Questions
 What makes Organic Krush different from other restaurants?
Organic Krush is built on the belief that food should fuel and nourish. Since 2015, we’ve crafted organic, sustainably sourced, and delicious food and juices with no GMOs, hormones, seed oils, or harmful pesticides so you can feel great about every bite and sip.
Do you offer vegan and gluten-free options?
Absolutely! Many of our items are naturally gluten-free, dairy-free, or plant-based, and we have plenty of vegetarian and vegan options! Our team is happy to help you swap ingredients or tailor meals to your preferences. Eating clean should still feel easy and satisfying.
 What types of events do you cater?
We cater gatherings of all sizes, from office/corporate lunches and team meetings to birthdays, wellness events, and large celebrations. Our menu includes bowls, wraps, salads, juices, smoothies, and gluten-free or plant-based options that make it easy to please everyone. Learn more about our catering here.
Do you deliver?
Yes! You can order delivery directly through our website here. We also partner with 3rd party delivery services like DoorDash, Uber Eats, and Grub Hub.
 What are cleanses, and how do they work?
Krush Cleanses are structured plans designed to help reset your body and support healthy habits. They can include cold-pressed juice cleanses, meal plans, and resets that aim to improve energy, digestion, immune function, and overall well-being without starvation or deprivation. Learn more about our cleanses here. We also offer meal plans for individual needs. 
How can I wholesale, partner, or collaborate with Organic Krush?
We love working with like-minded brands, influencers, wellness professionals, and local businesses. For catering partnerships, events, collaborations, or community opportunities, reach out to us at marketing@organickrush.com and our team will connect with you.
