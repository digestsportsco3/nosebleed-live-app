First Floor Commercial Space in Amagansett at 11 Indian Wells Hwy – The 2,480sf main level offers a highly adaptable commercial layout with a welcoming entry staircase from the sidewalk and a convenient side egress leading to a surprisingly expansive grassy yard—ideal for outdoor displays, seating, or activations.
