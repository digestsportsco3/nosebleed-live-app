Organic Cafe Taking Over Mary’s Marvelous Amagansett Location
 VIEWS
by Nicole Barylski
 in
 Real Estate 
 Home
 Real Estate
207 Main Street in Amagansett, the former home of Mary’s Marvelous, has sold. The one story building with 1100 sf and additional exterior storage space was listed for $575,000. 
“I would like to thank all of my loyal fans and patrons of the Mary’s Marvelous Amagansett store,” owner Mary Schoenlein shared on Mary’s Marvelous’ Facebook page. “It has been a pleasure to serve you for the past 15 years and I am deeply grateful for your business and support. I love the community and the town of Amagansett, so it is with sadness and regret that I say goodbye.”
Organic Krush, an organic cafe that offers 100 percent organic cold-pressed juice and food, will take over the prime Main Street space. Organic Krush was founded by the Paniccia, Tchinnis, and Walrath familes, who set out to bring healthy organic options to their family, friends and community. “We believe the health of our families is connected to the food we eat and the lifestyle we lead. Personally and professionally, we strive for balance, happiness and teamwork. You will see this approach in everything we do,” states the Organic Krush website.
However, Mary’s Marvelous fans like Alec Baldwin, Blythe Danner and Katie Joel will still be able to get their fix at the East Hampton location.
“I, and my staff, look forward to serving you there. Thank you again, for your tremendous loyalty, friendship and following,” Schoenlein. “I look forward to seeing you in East Hampton.”
