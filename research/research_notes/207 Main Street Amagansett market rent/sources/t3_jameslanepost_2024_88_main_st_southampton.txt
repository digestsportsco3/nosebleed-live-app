 Premier Retail & Office Space Hits Main Street In Southampton Village 
In the heart of Southampton Village, an iconic building stands tall, now offering a prime opportunity for lease. 88 Main Street, with its distinguished Colonial-style architecture, commands attention as one of the most prominent storefronts within the Village Shopping District.
Strategically positioned at the intersection of Main Street and Hampton Road, this property boasts visibility and access, making it an ideal location for retail or office ventures. Its historic charm combined with meticulous upkeep adds to its allure, promising a distinguished business address.
The interior layout is thoughtfully designed to accommodate various business needs. Current features include a welcoming reception area, two spacious executive offices, a well-appointed conference room, a designated print area, two convenient powder rooms, and ample open floor space adaptable for a range of purposes. Additionally, the internally accessed basement offers two finished rooms and a storage closet, further extending the potential utility of the space.
Details of the lease terms reveal a five-year lease with an extension option, presenting a long-term opportunity for prospective tenants. The base rent is set at $35,000 per month, with a total annual commitment of $360,000.
