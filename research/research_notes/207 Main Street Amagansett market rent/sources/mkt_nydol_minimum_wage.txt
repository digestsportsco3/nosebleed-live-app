 New York State Minimum Wage 

 Overview 

Everyone working in New York State is entitled to receive the New York State hourly minimum wage rates. 

This rate varies depending on your region, and the type of work that you do. The easiest way to find your rate is to use the Minimum Wage Lookup Tool.

 Minimum Wage Updates 

On January 1, 2027, New York’s minimum wage will stay the same. The minimum wage rates will be:
$17.00 per hour in New York City, Long Island, and Westchester County
$16.00 per hour in the rest of New York State
Explanation: Under New York Law, minimum wage rates can increase each year based on inflation. However, the law also includes certain conditions—known as “off-ramps”—that can prevent an automatic increase.
For 2027, the conditions for one of the off-ramps has been met. As a result, the minimum wage will not receive an inflation-based increase on January 1, 2027.
Exception: Home Care Minimum Wage
The minimum wage for home care aides will increase on January 1, 2027, to:
$20.00 per hour in New York City, Long Island, and Westchester County
$19.00 per hour in the rest of the state
Explanation: This is because home care minimum wage rates are adjusted for inflation but are capped at $3.00 above the standard minimum wage.
Governor Hochul’s Proposal
Governor Hochul is proposing a change to the law’s “off-ramp” provisions that would allow workers to receive the inflation-based wage increase. You can learn more on the Governor’s website.
