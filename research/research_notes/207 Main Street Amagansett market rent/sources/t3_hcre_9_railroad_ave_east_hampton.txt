Freestanding Luxury Retail Outlet in East Hampton 
9 Railroad Ave, East Hampton, New York 
Property Description 
One-of-a-kind retail outlet with character, offering a truly distinctive setting in the expanded East Hampton Village business district. Formerly an auto repair shop, this 3,000sf property has been meticulously renovated into a luxury retail gallery , blending its industrial charm with modern touches. High ceilings, large windows, and four garage roll doors create a spacious and versatile atmosphere, enhancing the shopping or gallery experience with a flexible indoor/outdoor feel.
The space is divided into three sections, allowing for a natural flow throughout, perfect for diverse uses including select retail, an art gallery , or even an office. Located adjacent to food shops, galleries, and surrounded by thriving local businesses, this site is ideal for a unique retailer seeking to make a mark in East Hampton. Ample parking is available, ensuring easy access for visitors, adding to the convenience of this property .
If you’re looking for a space with character, historical charm, and modern convenience, 9 Railroad Avenue is ready for its next occupant to bring it to life.
