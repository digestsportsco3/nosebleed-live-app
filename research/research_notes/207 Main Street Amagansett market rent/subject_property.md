- As of the 2018 use table, **restaurant and take-out food use are permitted as of right in CB**, though site-plan or Suffolk County Health Department sanitary capacity may still govern. The real constraint on food use is the **"wet use" scarcity**: limited sanitary flow and Health Department capacity in the hamlet, which makes an existing approved food-service space valuable. This supports a rent premium over "dry" retail.
- Historic-district status means façade, storefront, signage, lighting and awning changes need ARB review. Internally lit signs are disfavored. Original storefront and wood materials should be retained. That limits a tenant's ability to re-brand or modernize the exterior.
- On a ~2,178 SF lot the parcel is undersized versus the 3,000 SF CB minimum and appears to be at the 50% building-coverage cap. Expansion would likely require variances, so any change in use or intensification is constrained.
- On the "Coach Factory Store" name: the 2000 guidelines name each building's then-current occupant in parentheses (e.g., "Amagansett Wine and Spirits," "The Tack Trunk"). "The Coach Factory Store" was therefore the circa-2000 occupant of 207, before Mary's Marvelous opened in 2002. Earlier 2002 tenancy details are Unknown.
