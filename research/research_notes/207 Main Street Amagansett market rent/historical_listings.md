## 1. Was 207 Main Street itself ever publicly listed (2012–2026)? Ask, SF, lot, features, broker, outcome

### Takeaway
Yes. In late November/December 2016, Mary's Marvelous's leasehold at 207 Main Street was publicly marketed through Douglas Elliman as a lease assignment. The ask was **$625,000 in key money** for the long-term lease plus equipment. **Base rent was not disclosed.** The space was 1,100 SF, one story, with a kitchen, outdoor storage and a patio, on 0.05 acre. The ask was later reported at **$575,000**, and the lease sold to Organic Krush, closing **June 12, 2017**, after about 6–7 months on the market, at an undisclosed price. I found no public listing of the **fee (building) for sale**, and no other lease listing of the subject, in 2012–2026.

### Inferences
- The subject's own market evidence is a **leasehold premium (key money), not an asking rent**. $625,000 on 1,100 SF is about **$568/SF one-time** ($523/SF at $575K). Paying that much for an assignment implies the in-place contract rent was well below what the market would bear for a permitted wet-use Main Street space in 2016–17. Brokers framed the value as the scarce food-use permit plus the equipment.
- Time on market: listed about Nov/Dec 2016, closed 2017-06-12, so about **6–7 months**, through the off-season, with a possible 8% cut in the ask.
- The current $5,000/month ($60,000/yr) equals about **$54.55/SF/yr** on 1,100 SF. If this rent descends from the assigned lease, the 2017 key-money payment was the price for securing a below-market rent stream. This is inference only; the lease terms were never published.

### Gaps
- The base rent, term, expiry, renewal options and landlord identity of the lease assigned in 2017 are unknown, and none were published.
- The final assignment price is unknown (the buyer declined to disclose it).
- No Wayback capture of the Elliman listing page could be retrieved, so its listing date, photos and full terms were not verified.
- No fee-simple sale listing or recorded deed for 207 Main was found. PropertyShark and Suffolk County Clerk records would need paid or direct access.
- Curbed Hamptons coverage of the 2016–17 sale was not found. Curbed Hamptons had largely ceased by then; no result surfaced.

## 2. Key Amagansett comps: historical asks, dates, and what happened

### Takeaway
Few Amagansett spaces have **published historical asking rents**. Most press coverage reports sales, key-money asks or tenant turnover, not $/SF.

The dated Amagansett asking-rent points found are:
- **249 Main St**, 2021 pop-up: about $50K for the May–Sept season. The figure is ambiguous.
- **2095 Montauk Hwy (Napeague)**, Oct 2024: $180,000/yr for 4,476 SF, about $40/SF.
- **74A Montauk Hwy**, 2025–26: $240,000/yr NNN plus $700K key money, or $175,000 for the 2026 season, on 2,830 SF (about $85/SF annual).
- **136 Main Unit 1**, about 2025–26: $96,000/yr NNN for 500 SF ($192/SF).
- **11 Indian Wells Hwy**, about 2025–26: $35–$65/SF NNN.

The outcome pattern is turnover driven by sales and rent resets:
- Hampton Chutney left Amagansett Square in 2021 after a rent offer of about 3x; Doubles took the space.
- Indian Wells Tavern sold in 2020. The building was flipped in 2023 to investors, and Rowdy Hall opened there in 2023.
- Coche Comedor closed at 74A in Sept 2025, and the space was relisted for lease with key money.
- 519-521 Montauk Hwy has been vacant for years and on the market since 2023.

### Inferences
- **Key money is the main published price signal for Amagansett food space.** It appears at 207 Main ($625K, 2016) and at 74A Montauk Hwy ($700K, 2025–26). Wet-use permits are scarce, so incoming operators pay large one-time premiums. That points to contract rents that sit below what operators will ultimately pay for the right to occupy.
- **The published annual asks for restaurant space outside the Main Street core cluster around $40–$85/SF** (2095 Montauk Hwy at about $40 in 2024; 74A at about $85 in 2025–26). **Small Main Street retail asks run higher per SF** (136 Main Unit 1 at $192/SF for 500 SF). The subject's $54.55/SF sits at the low end of this range. It is consistent with a long-held, assigned lease and with key money having been paid.
- **Landlord rent resets drive turnover.** Examples: Outdoors (2012) was not renewed, Gone Local (2013) was evicted, Hampton Chutney (2021) faced about 3x rent, and Indian Wells Tavern (2020) closed when its building was sold. In each case the space re-let within months (Lazy Point/Ille Arts, Innersleeve Records, Doubles, Main Street Tavern/Rowdy Hall). The exception is 519-521 Montauk Hwy, which is outside the walkable core and has been vacant for years.
- The 177 Main Street sale history (bought for $2.825M in 2020, sold for $4.45M in 2023) shows Amagansett restaurant-building values up about 58% in under three years, after a renovation.

## 3. East Hampton Village and Montauk: dated asking-rent trend line (context)

### Takeaway
East Hampton Village prime retail asks were about **$250/SF around 2007–08**, fell to **$175–$200 by mid-2009**, and reached **$110–$140 for multiyear leases in 2011**. The average was about **$125/SF in early 2016**. Small Newtown Lane space hit **about $250/SF in 2023**. Current listings run about **$150–$245/SF** (another researcher covers current listings).

Southampton is a lower-tier comparison: about $110 early in 2019, cut to $85 (Main St) and $50 (Jobs Lane) by June 2019. Bridgehampton and Sag Harbor were around $50 in 2011. For Montauk, almost no dated $/SF lease asks were found. It shows deep discounting in 2009 (20–30% off prior-year asks) and large cuts to building sale asks in 2016–2018.

### Inferences

The larger restaurant boxes (74A at about $85; Napeague at about $40) sit far below.
